# Cellar Book

A private wine journal for family and friends.

**Open it:** https://liz5tone.github.io/Cellar-book/

- Log bottles with photos, ratings, critic scores, pairings and where you bought them.
- Every wine page takes on its region's colors and scenery.
- Browse bottles that Costco, Trader Joe's, Total Wine and Whole Foods regularly carry.
- See your regions on a zoomable world map.

## Journals and sharing

The bar under the logo shows which journal is open. Tap it to switch journals, start a **new empty journal**, rename one, or remove one from this phone.

Tap **Share** for two choices:

- **Send the app:** sends the link to Cellar Book. The other person starts with an empty journal of their own and never sees your wines.
- **Shared journal:** creates a journal you keep together with others, like a family cellar. Everyone who opens its link sees the same wines and can add, change and remove them. Your other journals stay private.

## Your journals are private

Private journals stay in your own browser on your own phone. Nothing is sent to a server, and nobody else who opens Cellar Book can see your wines.

A shared journal is the one exception. It's stored on the Cellar Book sharing server (`server/`) so everyone in it sees the same wines. Anyone who has its link can see and edit it.

Tap the status line under the logo (**Saved on this device ›**) to download a backup of the open journal now and then. Use **Restore from backup** on a new phone.

## Add it to your home screen

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen**.
