# Cellar Book

A private wine journal for family and friends.

**Open it:** https://liz5tone.github.io/cellar-book/

- Log bottles with photos, ratings, critic scores, pairings and where you bought them.
- Every wine page takes on its region's colors and scenery.
- Browse bottles that Costco, Trader Joe's, Total Wine and Whole Foods regularly carry.
- See your regions on a zoomable world map.

## Your journal is private

Everything you enter stays in your own browser on your own phone. Nothing is sent to a server, and nobody else who opens Cellar Book can see your wines.

Tap the status line under the logo (**Saved on this device ›**) to download a backup now and then. Use **Restore from backup** on a new phone.

## Add it to your home screen

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen**.

Map data: Natural Earth via world-atlas (public domain).
